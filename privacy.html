Cadence — Study Companion

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

The short version: Cadence does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data. Everything you create — study sessions, subjects, goals, assignments, streaks, and settings — stays on your device. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app works entirely offline.

Who this applies to

This policy applies to the Cadence iOS app (the “App”) published by the developer (“we”, “us”). If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We have no servers, no account system, and no way to identify you. We never see, receive, or store any data you enter into the App.

Data the App stores on your device

To function, the App saves the data you create locally on your device only. This includes:

  • Study sessions — durations, dates, and any notes you add (timer-based or manually logged).
  • Subjects — names, colors, icons, and weekly goals you set.
  • Assignments — titles, due dates, estimated effort, and completion status.
  • Goals & streaks — your daily goal and the streak history derived from your sessions.
  • App settings & preferences — session durations, sound/haptic preferences, chosen background, daily goal, headline-streak choice, and onboarding status.

This data is stored using Apple’s standard on-device storage technologies (SwiftData and UserDefaults). A small summary is also written to a private, on-device App Group container so the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and Live Activity widgets can display your stats. None of this data ever leaves your device through the App.

What we do not do

  • We do not transmit your data over the internet.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or usage-tracking tools.
  • We do not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not include third-party SDKs or trackers.
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone, because we never receive it.

Permissions the App may request

  • Notifications — If you allow them, the App schedules local notifications on your device to alert you when a focus or break phase ends. These are generated and delivered entirely by your device; nothing is sent to or through any server. Change or revoke this at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Cadence.
  • Live Activities / Dynamic Island — The App can display your running timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island using Apple’s ActivityKit. This runs on-device and shares no data externally. You can disable Live Activities in iOS Settings → Cadence.

The App does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location, or health data.

Device backups

If you back up your device (for example, via iCloud Backup or a computer), your App data may be included in that backup as part of the normal iOS backup process. Those backups are created and controlled by Apple and governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, not by us. We have no access to them.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are always in control of it. You can:

  • Delete individual subjects, assignments, or sessions inside the App.
  • Delete all App data at once by deleting the App from your device.

We retain nothing, because we never receive anything.

Security

Your data is protected by your device’s built-in security, including device passcode/biometric protection and iOS’s standard data protection. Because no data is transmitted off the device by the App, there is no transmission to intercept.

Children’s privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, no profiling, and no data collection of any kind.

Changes to this policy

If the App’s data practices change in the future (for example, if optional cloud sync is added), we will update this policy and revise the “Last updated” date above. Any new feature that involves transmitting data off your device will be clearly described here before it ships.
